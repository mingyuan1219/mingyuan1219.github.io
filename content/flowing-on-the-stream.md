This song was written in 2022 when I engaged with my partner. I probably would have never considered about marriage if I haven’t met him.

I knew nothing about singing at that time, but I want to sing.

Composed and sang by Ming on May 24th, 2022

## **Lyrics**

Fog is icy, breath in mist

Frosty droplets tap my lips

Sunlight runs through heavy clouds

Drop a light touch on my cheek

Sitting on a swaying boat

Flowing down a crystal stream

There is no sign to mark my pass

There is no map to show the way

I might flow to wonder land

I might flow to no man’s land

Either you will always be

Next to me

Birds are singing in the wind

It’s the first ring in the day

Water splashes on the boat

Catches our laugh in it

Stream is running through the mist

Neither of us can see its end

It’s a venture chose by us

No more need to hesitate

I might flow to wonder land

I might flow to no man’s land

Either you will always be

Next to me

We might flow to wonder land

We might flow to no man’s land

Either I will always be

Next to you

> **We need a witness to our lives. There’s a billion people on the planet… I mean, what does any one life really mean? But in a marriage, you’re promising to care about everything. The good things, the bad things, the terrible things, the mundane things… all of it, all of the time, every day. You’re saying ‘Your life will not go unnoticed because I will notice it. Your life will not go un-witnessed because I will be your witness’**
